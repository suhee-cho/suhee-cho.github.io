Suhee graduated from KAIST (Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology) with the highest honors (summa cum laude), ranking first in the Department of Bio and Brain Engineering. At the graduation ceremony, she delivered a speech as the representative of all graduating students across bachelor’s, master’s, and doctoral programs. Her speech was presented to an audience of over 10,000 people, including the President of South Korea, the President of KAIST, and fellow graduates.

She was also selected as a recipient of the KAIST Leadership Award, having accumulated the highest number of leadership points among undergraduate students in her graduating class.
